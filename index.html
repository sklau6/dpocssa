01 — Platform portfolio

Two centralised services, one operating standard.

Both platforms are shared infrastructure: the same support discipline, change control and reporting applied to conversational AI and to social text analytics.

Conversational AI

Chatbot-as-a-Service

A multi-tenant platform that lets bureaux and departments publish governed assistants over their own service content, without building a conversational stack each time.

  • Intent, entity and dialogue design with versioned configuration
  • Retrieval-augmented answers over curated, approved corpora
  • Bilingual delivery — Traditional Chinese and English
  • Guardrails, escalation to human channels, and refusal handling
  • Channel connectors: web, kiosk and messaging surfaces
  • Conversation analytics, containment and satisfaction reporting

02 — Scope of work

Seven duties, six operating pillars.

The brief describes a role that sits between engineering, service management and the business — close enough to the platforms to fix them, close enough to B/Ds to make them usable.

Build & run

Support design, implementation, testing, maintenance and administration of the centralised AI and analytics platforms.

  • Release and regression testing before change windows
  • Configuration, prompt and taxonomy version control
  • Health monitoring, patching and capacity checks

Adoption & enablement

Promote the platforms, arrange adoption and provide technical and user support to government B/Ds.

  • Briefings, demos and onboarding sessions
  • User support, triage and knowledge articles
  • Usage reporting back to service owners

Coordination

Keep contractors, internal teams, consulting teams and B/Ds aligned so information flows without gaps.

  • Single point of contact for delivery questions
  • Action logs, decisions and escalation paths
  • Handover packs between teams and shifts

Research & insight

Conduct market research and analysis on Gen AI and data analytics developments relevant to government use.

  • Vendor and model landscape scanning
  • Evaluation criteria, risks and guardrail practice
  • Short written briefs for decision makers

Records & knowledge

Maintain electronic and physical files so documents stay current, accurately recorded and easy to find.

  • Folder structures, naming and version discipline
  • Retention, access and disposal aligned to policy
  • Runbooks and decision records kept current

Resilience

Provide technical and administrative support outside normal hours for special events, fire-fighting and urgent deployment.

  • On-call rota and escalation contacts
  • Pre-event readiness checks and rehearsals
  • Incident logging and post-incident review
After-hours cover is part of the role.

Special events, urgent service deployments and live incidents can require support outside office hours. The expectation is not permanent availability, but a rehearsed, documented response when the call comes — and a clean handover back to the normal rota afterwards.

04 — Operating model

How the platforms are actually run.

Three support tiers, severity-based response windows, and a cadence that keeps B/Ds, vendors and internal teams looking at the same numbers.

L1

Service desk

First contact for B/D users. Access requests, how-to questions, known issues and ticket hygiene.

  • Ticket logging and classification
  • Known-error and knowledge base
  • Escalation to L2 with evidence
L2

Platform team (this role)

Configuration, content, data and model issues. Diagnosis across platform, tenancy and integration.

  • Root-cause analysis and workarounds
  • Configuration and prompt changes
  • Vendor escalation with reproduction
L3

Vendor engineering

Product defects, model or infrastructure faults, and fixes that require source-level change.

  • Defect reproduction and fixes
  • Patch and upgrade delivery
  • Contractual SLA accountability

06 — Governance

Controls that travel with AI in government.

Supporting AI platforms in a government context means the running system and the paperwork around it have to agree with each other.

Security & data classification

Data handled by bots and analytics feeds is classified before it is ingested; controls follow the classification, not convenience.

Access & least privilege

Tenant roles scoped to need, recertified on a schedule, and reviewed after every join, move or departure.

Records & retention

Electronic and physical files kept current, correctly named, access-controlled and disposed of on schedule.

Vendor & contract control

Deliverables tied to milestones and measurable service levels, with acceptance evidence filed as it is produced.

Model quality assurance

Evaluation sets, drift checks and fallback behaviour reviewed before release and after any model change.

Continuity & escalation

Runbooks, contact trees and rehearsed failover paths for events where the platform simply must hold.

Operational checklist

  • Change record approved before configuration, prompt or taxonomy deployment
  • Rollback tested for every material release
  • Incident log updated through to closure, including workaround and root cause
  • B/D communications sent within the severity window
  • Access recertification completed and evidenced
  • Documentation and runbooks current after every change
  • Vendor SLA evidence collected monthly, not reconstructed at year end

08 — Research & roadmap

Market watch, translated into platform work.

Research is only useful if it changes something. Each theme below ends in a decision the platform team can act on within the year.

  1. Q1

    Evaluation & guardrails

    Build a repeatable evaluation set for both platforms and agree refusal, escalation and citation behaviour.

    Output: evaluation harness, release gate
  2. Q2

    Retrieval quality

    Improve grounding of answers: chunking, metadata filters and freshness checks on approved corpora.

    Output: retrieval benchmarks, content refresh cycle
  3. Q3

    Bilingual depth

    Test Traditional Chinese and English parity across intents, plus code-mixed social text handling.

    Output: bilingual test suite, taxonomy update
  4. Q4

    Analytics depth

    Move from volume and sentiment to issue detection with lead time, and expose trend evidence to B/Ds.

    Output: issue alerting playbook

On the radar

  • Agentic workflows and tool use under constrained permissions
  • Smaller, domain-tuned models for on-premise inference
  • Retrieval evaluation and citation quality as a release criterion
  • AI governance frameworks, model cards and audit trails
  • Synthetic data for evaluation where real data cannot be used

09 — Questions

Before you apply.

Is this a development role or a support role?

Primarily support and operations, with development involvement. The brief covers design, implementation and testing, but the centre of gravity is keeping two live platforms running and adopted.

Which platforms are in scope?

Two centralised services: Chatbot-as-a-Service and the Social Media Text Analytics System. Both are shared across bureaux and departments rather than owned by a single team.

How much vendor work is involved?

Substantial. Contractors build and support the platforms, so a large share of the role is specifying, chasing and verifying vendor delivery against milestones and service levels.

What does after-hours support mean in practice?

Cover during special events, urgent deployments and live incidents, on a rota rather than permanently. Readiness checks beforehand and a clean handover afterwards are part of the expectation.

What experience is mandatory?

Three or more years in each of four bands: IT application development and management; system implementation and maintenance/support; management and support of government systems and services; and vendor management.
